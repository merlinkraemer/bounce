# Privacy

Bounce collects no data.

- No accounts.
- No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting.
- No ads and no third-party services.
- Your tracks, versions, notes and playlists stay on your own devices.

## Network

Bounce talks only to your own devices on your local network. It uses Bonjour to find your paired Mac or iPhone and sends audio files directly between them. Nothing is sent to the internet and nothing is uploaded to a server. This is why the apps ask for local network access.

## Microphone

If a voice-note feature is present in your version, the iPhone app asks for microphone access only to record it. Recordings are stored on your device and are never sent anywhere except to your paired Mac over the local network.

## Your data

Everything is stored on your Mac and your iPhone. Deleting the apps and their data removes it. Pairing keys are stored in the system keychain.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue in the project repository.
